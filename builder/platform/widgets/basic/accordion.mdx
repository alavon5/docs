Accordion widgets are perfect for **FAQs or sections where you need to add more text** without taking up too much space.

* **Navigate to the Accordion Widget** – Open the **Widgets panel** in your editor.
* **Drag & Drop onto Your Page** – Place it in the desired section.
* **Expand & Collapse** – Users can click to reveal or hide content, ensuring a **clean and organized layout**.

A great way to keep your pages **structured and user-friendly**!

![](/images/builder/957f4c4d658e866f.png)

### Accordion styling

Currently there are four different layout designs you can chose from. You can find them in the styling tab

![](/images/builder/2388eb3134f7da74.png)

### Font and colour styling

The same with any of our elements you can style the design to how your prefer. To start editing the styling for your accordion, simply select the accordion settings tab. Then a new window will display:

![](/images/builder/8f3cb5b24c42eab1.png)

From here you can go into the settings cog and style the font, font sizing, adding a call to action link and also the box colouring.

![](/images/builder/b3a3342d6cf2725e.png)

This element layers text to create a dynamic, eye-catching headline. You can pair a bold, static headline (like "Your Headline Goes Here") with a second layer of large, faded background text that scrolls horizontally behind or alongside it, or use the scrolling text on its own for a marquee-style effect.

![](/images/builder/2b64779589d29746.png)

#### **Why use it:**

* **Grabs attention:** movement naturally draws the eye more than static text, making it great for hero sections or banners.
* **Adds depth and personality:** the layered look (bold foreground text over soft, oversized scrolling text) gives pages a modern, editorial feel without needing custom graphics.
* **Reinforces your message:** use the scrolling layer to repeat a keyword or phrase (like "Inspire") for emphasis, while the static headline delivers the actual message.
* **Flexible use:** works as a decorative background effect, a standalone scrolling banner, or a combined two-layer design depending on what the section needs.

### **How it works:**

Open the scrolling text settings by clicking the edit button. Next you will see the editing canvas display. Select your layout design from:

* **Main text + big scrolling text behind**
* **Scrolling text only**

![](/images/builder/ce1a5c59069a12b5.png)

### **Main text + Big scrolling text behind**

This display option enables you to have a layered text effect, while the main text remains static and the scrolling text is neatly positioned behind, giving you an amazing display on your page. 

### **Scrolling text only**

This display option allows your to just display a banner of scrolling text across your pages.

![](/images/builder/cdfca3a35b334090.png)

With both display styles, you can increase, or decrease the speed in which the text scroll time taken to reach one side of the page to the other..

To customize your **favicon** and **social share image**:

* **Go to Settings** > Select the **General** tab.
* **Upload Your Favicon** – This small icon appears in the browser tab to help users identify your site.
* **Set Your Social Share Image** – This defines the preview image shown when your website is shared on social media.

These elements enhance **branding and visibility**, ensuring your site looks professional across platforms!

### **What is a social share image?**

The social share image will be displayed to the public when your website or website pages are shared. For example on social media. 

<Note>
**Note:** You to have social share images for every page and also funnel step on your website.
</Note>

<Tip>
**Top tip**: Create individual images for each of your pages, this will enable people to quickly identify what the page is about. 
</Tip>

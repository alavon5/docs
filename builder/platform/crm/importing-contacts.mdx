Bringing in existing contacts helps **populate your account efficiently** with all your past and current details.

* **Go to the "More" Tab** – Locate the additional options in your account dashboard.
* **Select "Import"** – Choose this from the **drop-down menu** to begin the process.
* **Upload Your Contact List** – Ensure data is formatted properly for a smooth transition.

A quick way to keep your contacts organized and readily accessible!

![](/images/builder/918fd6677758d8a7.png)

Now a new window will open within the import section. To import your contacts - select Import now and simply add your CSV file containing your client information and that's it. All your contacts will now be automatically added to your Contacts area.

![](/images/builder/4a59ded3d7582f7b.png)

Ensure proper attribution by defining the **author name** in your blog post settings.

**How to Set the Author Name:**

1. **Navigate to 'Post Author'** – Found in the post settings menu.
2. **Enter the Author's Name** – Specify the individual who wrote the article.
3. **Save Your Changes** – Ensure the correct name is displayed for attribution.

A **simple step** to establish **credibility and transparency** in your blog!

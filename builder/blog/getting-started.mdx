Getting started on your blog is simple!

**Steps to Begin:**

1. **Navigate to the "Pages" Menu** – Find the section where all website pages are managed.
2. **Click on "Blog"** – Open the blog setup and editing interface.
3. **Customize Your Blog Layout** – Adjust design, settings, and organization.
4. **Start Adding Posts** – Write engaging content to attract and retain readers.

With **just a few clicks**, you’ll be on your way to building a valuable blog!

![](/images/builder/79a6ccac656c6db9.png)

This will now open the main blog listing page where you can add new blogs and more.

![](/images/builder/2d0a3c4e3e2db9bd.png)

**From here, you can:**

* Filter to see All, Published, Drafts, or Trash articles.
* [Edit the Header & Sidebar for the blog](/builder/blog/getting-started/blog-header-and-sidebar-settings)
* Add posts
* Delete selected posts
* Change the Blog Settings
* See the list of all your articles including comments, author and published date
* Clone an article
* Preview and article

Tailor the **automatic emails** sent from your system to match your brand’s communication style.

**Steps to Customize System Emails:**

* **Go to "Email and Automations"** – Access the section where email settings are managed.
* **Click on "System Emails"** – Locate the templates for automated messages.
* **Select the Email Template** – Choose the email you want to modify.
* **Disable the Default Email** – Opt to send your own customized version instead.

This ensures **greater personalization and flexibility** in your email communication!

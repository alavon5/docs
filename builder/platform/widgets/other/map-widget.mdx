#### **Embedding a Map on Your Website**

Integrating a **map widget** makes it easy for visitors to find locations directly on your site.

* **Go to Widgets** – Open the **Widgets panel** in your editor.
* **Navigate to "Other Widgets"** – Scroll through additional customization options.
* **Select the Map Widget** – Drag and drop it onto your page for a seamless display.

A great way to **improve accessibility** and enhance user experience!

### **Editing your map**

Simply type in your location, set your display and you're ready to go.

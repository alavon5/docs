#### **Creating Tags to Segment Your Audience**

Tags are a great way to **organize and categorize your contacts** for better audience segmentation.

* **Go to the Contacts Tab** – Open your contacts management section.
* **Select the Tab Icon** – Navigate to the tagging feature to create new tags.
* **Apply Tags Strategically** – Use them to **group contacts by interest, purchase behavior, or engagement level**.

This makes it easier to manage your audience and tailor communication effectively!

![](/images/builder/b2aecfe10f67fe12.png)

Once you created a new tag it will display in your tags list. You will also be able to view all contacts who have a specific tag added to their profiles

<Note>
You can also create tags on various widgets such as checkouts, forms, trigger automations and more. To help speed up your tag creating process.
</Note>

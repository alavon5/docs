Our **Review Widget** is a powerful tool designed to streamline user feedback and engagement on your platform. With a **customizable 5-star rating system**, members can easily rate their experiences and share detailed reviews.

**Key Features:**

* **Customizable Review Forms** – Define the structure of your review process.
* **Approval Controls** – Manage new reviews with optional approval settings.
* **Threaded Replies** – Enable structured discussions within reviews.
* **Image Attachments** – Allow users to enhance feedback with images.
* **Styling Options** – Adjust colors, backgrounds, and display layouts.
* **Light & Dark Theme Support** – Ensure accessibility and branding consistency.

Administrators have full control over **comment order, permissions, and visibility**, ensuring a seamless integration that aligns with your community’s needs. Whether gathering insights, boosting engagement, or refining interactions, the **Review Widget** creates a dynamic and trustworthy feedback experience.

<Tip>
**Drag & Drop** – Easily position the review widget within pages, funnels, to discussion threads.
</Tip>

### Create Your Review

To begin setting up your **Review Widget**, follow these steps:

1. **Navigate to the left-side task bar** and locate the **Review Widget icon**.
2. **Drag & drop** the widget into the **builder canvas**.
3. **Start editing the settings** to customize how reviews are displayed and collected.

Once placed, you can refine details like **approval settings, comment visibility, and styling preferences**

![](/images/builder/de709dd45753ec54.png)

### Enter Name

To make your **Review Widget** easily reusable across different pages, blogs, and projects, you can assign it a **custom name or label**.

* **Define a unique name** – Helps you quickly identify and reuse the widget.
* **Consistency across content** – Keeps your branding and review structure uniform.
* **Simplifies future integration** – Easily drop the widget into new sections without reconfiguring settings.

This allows for seamless **content management** while ensuring a **consistent experience** wherever your reviews are placed.

### Require Approval on New Reviews

To ensure quality control over user feedback, you can require **approval** before new reviews are published.

**How It Works:**

* **Enable Approval** – Activate this setting to review submissions before they go live.
* **Admin Control** – Except, delete in your moderator dashboard.
* **Maintain Review Integrity** – Filter out irrelevant or inappropriate content before it's visible to users.

This feature helps keep your platform **organized, professional, and aligned with community standards**.

[Community Moderation](/builder/community-lms/create-a-community/community-moderation)

### Who can Reply

If **require approval on new replies** is enabled, any **member-submitted comments to a review** will require **admin approval** before being published.

**How It Works:**

* **Comments are queued** in the **moderation dashboard**.
* **Admins review and approve** each comment before it becomes visible.
* **Rejected comments** will not be displayed to users.
* **Ensures content quality** and prevents spam or inappropriate engagement.

This feature provides greater **control over discussions**, keeping review interactions professional and relevant. 

### Allow Images

You can enable **image uploads** for both **reviewers** and **members replying to reviews**, adding more depth to feedback.

**How It Works:**

* **Reviewers** – Can include images to support their review.
* **Replying Members** – Can attach images within their responses.
* **Moderation Controls** – Admins can **approve or delete** if needed.

This feature enhances **engagement and clarity**, making feedback more visual and informative.

![](/images/builder/e46ead15d864a7b0.png)<p>Member Review with Image Attached</p>

### Order

You can control the order in which reviews are displayed within your page, ensuring the flows in a way that best suits your setup.

* **Ascending Order** – Displays the **oldest reviews first**, maintaining a structured timeline from earliest to latest.
* **Descending Order** – Shows the **newest reviews first**, keeping the most recent discussions at the top for immediate visibility.

Adjusting this setting allows you to **prioritize engagement**, whether you want to highlight fresh interactions or maintain chronological readability.

### Initial Number of Reviews to Display 

This straightforward setting allows you to **control how many reviews** are visible by default before members need to load more.

* Choose to display **3, 5, 10, or even 20** reviews initially.
* Ensures a **clean and organized** layout.

This feature keeps engagement flexible while allowing for a streamlined experience.

### **Light Theme for Text & Backgrounds**

This setting is designed to enhance readability and contrast when using **Dark Mode** or a **darker-colored background**.

**How It Works:**

* **Improves Visibility** – Ensures reviews stand out with crisp, readable text.
* **Enhances Design Harmony** – Helps reviews **blend into your layout** while maintaining accessibility.
* **Custom Styling Options** – Adjust text brightness and background transparency for **optimal contrast**.

With this feature, your **reviews remain visually clear** while complementing your overall design aesthetic.

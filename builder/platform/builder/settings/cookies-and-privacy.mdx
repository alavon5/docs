Activating a cookie disclaimer is **quick and straightforward**:

* **Go to Settings** – Navigate to the **Cookies & Privacy** section.
* **Enable the Cookie Disclaimer** – Toggle the setting to display a disclaimer on your website.

This ensures compliance with privacy regulations while informing visitors about **cookie usage**.

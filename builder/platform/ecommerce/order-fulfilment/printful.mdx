The **Printfu**l integration lets you design, sell, and fulfil print-on-demand products automatically so you can focus on creating and marketing, not production or shipping.

When a customer places an order in your store, it’s sent directly to Printful for printing and fulfillment. Printful handles production, packing, and shipping, while order and tracking details stay in sync with your store.

To get connected open your Printful dashboard and select the **more tab** > **Store Settings** > **API**

![](/images/builder/038955ede5b5b4cb.png)

Next - Select your store you wan to use from the Choose store dropdown menu. Then click the red '**Go to Printful Developers**' button:

![](/images/builder/1977a2a777bf4c09.png)

Next select the **Tokens tab** to the left and choose the **Private Token** option > **Create a token**

![](/images/builder/070c38e75aafc82b.png)

**Add new token settings:**

* Name your token for reference
* Add your contact email address
* Expiration date for your token
* Configure your access level
* Scopes - Which permissions you wish to grant

![](/images/builder/3a0cbd5d3d759320.png)

Once your add new token settings are complete, click the **Create new token** button.

A new tab will display below with your private access Key: Copy that key...

![](/images/builder/ba5674978897468c.png)

Once you have copied that API Key, navigate back to your ecommerce store and **select settings** > **Add integration** and **select Printful**:

![](/images/builder/1cb97f41682f1fec.png)

Upon adding your Printful API Key, you will now see a new display. This is where you can start mapping your store products to your existing Printful products.

First, select the **Map Products** button. This will open a new display:

![](/images/builder/39238dc64dcf57ed.png)

The left side column is your ecommerce store items, and to the right is where you map your prinful items. Simply scroll to the items you wish to connect and ensure you map the correct items in the Printful side.

Once you are happy that you have mapped the correct products, select the Save mappings button. This will now display your product as mapped:

Once mapped, you can go ahead and more more items if required.

![](/images/builder/f6e976b88a80cf7b.png)

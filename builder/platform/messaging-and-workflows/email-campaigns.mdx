### **Campaign settings**

Craft **catchy subject lines** to **boost open rates** and personalize them using CRM properties.

**Best Practices:**

* **Make It Intriguing** – Spark curiosity or urgency (e.g., *"Your Exclusive Offer Expires Soon!"*).
* **Personalize with CRM Data** – Use the subscriber’s **name or recent activity** for relevance.
* **Keep It Short & Clear** – Aim for **50 characters or less** for better mobile readability.

**Test Before Sending:**

* **Send a Test Email** – Check formatting, **links, and design** before launching your campaign.

A **strategic approach** to ensure **higher engagement and conversions!** 

![](/images/builder/970bdfb2dbd2c05f.png)

## **Email insights**

Once your campaign has successfully been sent to your subscribers, you can now view the email performance. Simply click on the campaign you want to view:

![](/images/builder/03db9b44fc5f7cbe.png)

To analyse your email campaign performance, this dashboard will help you view:

* Email opens
* Link clicks
* Preview - so you can remember what the email contained
* Who unsubscribed
* and more...

### Individual subscriber performance

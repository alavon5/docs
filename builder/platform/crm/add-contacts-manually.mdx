Effortlessly add contacts to your system for better organization and personalization.

* **Select "Add Contact"** – Located at the **top right** side of the dashboard.
* **Enter Details** – Add a **name and email address** to create the entry.
* **Enhance Contact Profiles** – Apply **tags, allocate membership data**, and more for streamlined management.

This ensures a **well-structured contact list** tailored to your needs!

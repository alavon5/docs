Ensure **exclusive content visibility** by limiting access to specific membership groups.

* **Go to "Pages" or Locate the Folder** – Find the page you want to restrict.
* **Click the Three Dots Icon** – Access the **page settings**.
* **Select "Members Only"** – Choose this option from the **Page Access dropdown**.
* **Click "Choose Member Groups"** – Assign the groups that should have **access**.
* **Repeat for Funnel Steps** – The same process applies to funnel steps to **control user access efficiently**.

A great way to protect premium content while maintaining structured membership accessibility!

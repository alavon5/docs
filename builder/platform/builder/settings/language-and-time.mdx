To adjust your website’s **language translations** (for store, blog, etc.) and **timezone settings**:

* **Go to Settings** – Navigate to the **Language & Time** section.
* **Select Your Preferred Language** – This will update translations across your platform.
* **Adjust Timezone** – Ensures content and scheduling align with your local time.

This helps keep your site **accurate and accessible** for users in different regions!

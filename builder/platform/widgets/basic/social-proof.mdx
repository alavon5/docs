This element combines profile photos, a star rating, and a trust statement into one compact, credibility-boosting widget, perfect for placing near a headline, CTA, or hero section where visitors are deciding whether to trust you.

![](/images/builder/292aaa6a73e7953c.png)

#### **Why use it.**

* **Builds instant trust:** real (or representative) faces paired with a high rating create a fast, human signal that others already use and like your product.
* **Reduces hesitation:** placed near a signup form or CTA, it reassures visitors right at the moment they're deciding to act.
* **Lightweight and flexible:** no need for full testimonials or case studies; a few avatars and a number communicate credibility in a single glance.
* **Customizable messaging:** the "Text below" field lets you reinforce specific proof points (e.g., "Trusted by 8,000+ enterprise teams") rather than a generic rating alone.

### **Settings available:**

* **Show profile images:** toggle avatar stack on/off, add/remove/reorder images
* **Show rating number:** toggle the numeric score and star display
* **Rating / Score:** the number shown (e.g., 4.9)
* **Rating label:** text next to the score (e.g., "rating")
* **Text below:** supporting trust statement under the rating
* **Stars color / Rating & text color:** customize colors to match your brand
* **Link:** make the whole element clickable (e.g., link to reviews page)

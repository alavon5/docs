If you're selling **physical store items**, enabling **custom shipping settings** ensures smooth delivery management.

* **Go to the Shipping Tab** – Access your shipping configurations from the main dashboard.
* **Adjust Personalized Shipping Options** – Set rules **based on price, weight, or location**.
* **Apply Global or Local Rules** – Define shipping policies **for specific regions or worldwide**.

A great way to keep **logistics streamlined and customer experience optimized!**

![](/images/builder/372850bd4ff07520.png)

Now to set up your shipping option requirements:

Create shipping rules based on:

* Price range
* Weight range

Then set up which Countries you want to apply the specific shipping rules to.

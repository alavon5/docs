In addition to managing store products from the **store tab**, you can also **create products** directly while working on your funnels.

* **Navigate to the Store Tab** – Open the store section in your editor.
* **Select "Create New Product"** – Start building your product without leaving your funnel setup.

This streamlines product creation, letting you **seamlessly integrate new offerings** while optimizing your funnel for conversions!

![](/images/builder/4ad965b58270b4cc.png)

Once you have selected the create new product a side window will open. The process of creating the store product is the exact as you would within the store tab.

​

![](/images/builder/da5879b2924b94be.png)

<Note>
Once you have created your product, it will be automatically added to your store as normal. So you can use it wherever you need to.
</Note>

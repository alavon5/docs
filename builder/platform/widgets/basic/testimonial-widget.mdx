This element showcases customer reviews and quotes, complete with star ratings, photos, names, and roles, to build trust and social proof. It supports three distinct layouts, so you can match the presentation to the section's purpose and available space.

![](/images/builder/d953cc4f72281b4b.png)

#### **Why use it**

* **Builds credibility:** real names, roles, and companies (e.g., "Sophia Bennett, Product Designer, Lumen") make praise feel authentic rather than generic.
* **Star ratings add instant visual trust:** visitors can gauge sentiment before reading a word.
* **Flexible presentation:** choose the layout that fits the section: a compact rotating slider, a scannable list, or a full grid of reviews.

### **Layout Options**

**Slider:** shows one testimonial at a time with navigation arrows and dots. Great for tight spaces (like next to a hero image) where you want to feature quotes without taking up much room. Includes **Slider Type** options: Single slide, Multiple slides, or Auto rotate (no controls) for a hands-free carousel.

![](/images/builder/8db97401eae78ea6.png)

**List:** stacks testimonials vertically in full-width rows. Ideal for dedicated testimonial sections where you want visitors to read through several reviews in sequence.

![](/images/builder/9051e200dc7236b7.png)

**Grid:** displays testimonials side-by-side in columns. Best for showing multiple reviews at once and reinforcing volume ("lots of happy customers"). **Items per row** lets you control how many display at once.

![](/images/builder/1c017b2f1cf8c642.png)

To set or change the URL of your blog article/post, go to the **'Post URL'** section in the [post settings menu](/builder/blog/article-settings).

![](/images/builder/bdc68026b9c8ea93.png)

The **slug** is the final section of your post’s URL, typically reflecting the **article name or title** for easy readability.

**How to Add a Slug:**

1. **Navigate to the URL Slug Field** – Located in your post settings.
2. **Enter a Clear, Relevant Slug** – Keep it concise and related to the article topic.
3. **Example:**
   * If you set your slug as `"my-awesome-article"`
   * Your full post URL will be: `yourwebsite.com/blog/my-awesome-article`

A **simple way** to create **SEO-friendly URLs** that improve navigation and search visibility!

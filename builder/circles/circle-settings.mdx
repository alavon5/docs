To start editing your Circle, navigate to the Community tab in the top task bar and if you have already started to create a Circle you can select it below. If not go ahead and create your first[ circle](/builder/community-lms/create-a-community#create-a-new-circle).

![](/images/builder/748b5f00d34bfb6a.png)

Within this dashboard you can view the **top level settings**, located to the right side of the Circle, here you can:

**Duplicate**: If you want to quickly duplicate an existing circle wit hall content included.

**Preview**: Live preview your Circle 

**Copy Link**: A URL that you can share 

**Unpublish**: This will remove your Circle from the public and members

**Delete**: Deleting your Circle is permanent - So only delete when you are 100% you need it deleting.

### Accessing your Circle

To access any of your Circles, simply hover over the one you wan to access and left click.

Craft compelling posts with ease using the **drag-and-drop builder**—just like the page builder!

**How to Structure Your Post:**

* **Locate the Summary Field** – Positioned above the main content builder.
* **Use the Drag-and-Drop Builder** – Add blocks, columns, and widgets effortlessly.
* **Customize Your Layout** – Arrange content dynamically for an engaging design.
* **Access the Widget Menu** – Choose elements that enhance readability and functionality.

A **versatile way** to design **rich, interactive blog articles** with minimal effort!

<Note>
You can also use the A.I writing assistant to create it for you. Read more [here](/builder/platform/ai-writing-assistant#blog-ai)
</Note>

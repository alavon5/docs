**Fixes:**

* **Automations:** Resolved error when workflows exceeded **100 steps**

* **Checkout:** Customers can no longer buy **only the bump offer** when multiple pre-selected items exist in checkout
* **Email Marketing:** **Brand details** now appear in the footer of **double opt-in emails**

**General Improvements:**

* **System stability & performance upgrades** for smoother functionality
* **Updated translations** across various languages for better localization

The **Orders Tab** provides a **comprehensive overview** of all transactions within your storefront and specific funnels.

**Key Features:**

* **View & Edit Orders** – Track incoming purchases and make necessary modifications.
* **Archived Orders** – Store completed transactions for future reference.
* **Abandoned Cart** – Monitor users who left items in their cart without purchasing.
* **Paid Status Changes** – Update payment confirmations or adjust status as needed.
* **Order Status** – Keep track of fulfillment progress and customer notifications.
* **Export Orders** – Generate reports for bookkeeping or further analysis.

This **streamlined order management system** ensures a **smooth and efficient shopping experience!**

![](/images/builder/2355bb8e9c27e5e4.png)

### **Orders**

The orders tab show a list of people who have purchased from you. Here you can also select and update order status. You can also select the customer to view their profile via the CRM. 

![](/images/builder/0699d2dc8fd3c949.png)

You will notice an actions tab that is located next to the invoice number '**Invoice #201**' here are some new options you can use:

1. **+ Add new item**
2. **Archive order**
3. **Send order details to customer**
4. **Print**

![](/images/builder/a4df4b1a6eb07411.png)

1\. **Add new item** - You can now add more items to the users profile and will be added to their existing order ready for a payment email to be automatically sent.

**Adding a new item:** 

Select add new item > Select product from list > Change the qty and price if required > Now the item will be added to the customers profile as shown below

![](/images/builder/161a10f0ee1a0cb3.png)

You can now send an email to the customer using the blue button - Send order update email

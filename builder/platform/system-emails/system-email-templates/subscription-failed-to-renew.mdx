Maintain a professional tone while **notifying users** about failed subscription renewals using the **drag-and-drop email editor** for full customization.

**What’s Included in the Default Template:**

* **System Fields** – Website name and allocated subscription details.
* **Pre-set Content** – Renewal failure notification and next steps for resolution.

**How to Customize:**

* **Modify or Remove Fields** – Adjust placeholders to match your messaging preferences.
* **Use the Drag-and-Drop Editor** – Personalize layout, colors, and formatting effortlessly.
* **Refine Messaging** – Ensure clarity while providing actionable solutions.

A **simple yet effective way** to **enhance user communication** while ensuring a seamless renewal process!

![](/images/builder/e2cedf3013bffa4b.png)

To add any further fields to the system email template you can do so by selecting the text editor when typing and click the **Tag** icon. The tag icon will then display the specific fields you can add to the system template as shown below.

![](/images/builder/1f113bd557cebcba.png)

Here you will see all the specific fields allocated to the subscription failed to renew system email.

Also all CRM [properties](/builder/platform/crm/properties) that can be added to the email too. Note if you have any custom properties you have created they will be listed here too.

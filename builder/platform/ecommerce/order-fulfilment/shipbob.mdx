Outsource fulfilment without losing control. The **ShipBob** integration connects your store directly to ShipBob’s global fulfilment network, allowing you to store inventory, automate order fulfilment, and deliver faster to your customers.

Orders placed in your store are automatically sent to ShipBob for picking, packing, and shipping no manual steps required. With distributed warehouses and real-time syncing, this integration is built for brands ready to scale.

Watch ShipBob's tutorial - **How to create your API Token**:

[Watch the video](https://youtu.be/pDTfDSts4W0)

Once you have created your ShipBob API token, navigate back to your ecommerce store tab and **select settings** > **Add Integration** > **Select ShipBob**

![](/images/builder/f8e41ce2844f586b.png)

Now paste in your ShipBob API Key in the pop out display

![](/images/builder/629d10f53783a2ea.png)

That's your ecommerce store and ShipBob now fully linked together.

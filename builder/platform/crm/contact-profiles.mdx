Each user has their own **profile**, where you can handle various settings to streamline engagement and organization.

* **Add Tags** – Categorize users for better segmentation.
* **Update Properties** – Modify key details for personalization.
* **Add to Member Groups** – Organize users into relevant communities.
* **Delete Contacts** – Remove outdated or unnecessary profiles.
* **Reset Passwords** – Provide account access when needed.
* **Form Submit Overview** – Track user form submissions.
* **Orders** – View and manage purchase history.
* **Email Marketing Info** – Analyze engagement with marketing emails.
* **Send Emails** – Directly communicate with users from their profile.

A centralized approach for **efficient user management**!

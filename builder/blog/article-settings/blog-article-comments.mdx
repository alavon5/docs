Encourage engagement by allowing readers and community members to interact with your content. Activate the **comment feature** in your blog settings to foster discussion and feedback.

#### **How to Enable Comment Options**

* There are two display formats to choose from
* **Legacy Comments** – A traditional comment section offering a familiar structure
* **Comment Widget** – A modern, customizable option designed for seamless integration

![](/images/builder/f1f39b077bf07c8b.png)

### **Legacy Comments:** 

Legacy comments allow **website visitors** to engage with your blog posts **without needing to log in**. Readers can leave feedback **with or without providing an email address**, making it a seamless way to encourage discussion.

#### **Managing Legacy Comments**

Approving comments is **optional** if enabled, you can moderate interactions directly from your **blog settings display**.

**How to Manage Comments:**

* **Navigate to the 'Comments' Section** – Located at the bottom of the post settings menu.
* **View Existing Comments** – Monitor interactions from readers.
* **Approve Comments** – Publish feedback that adds value.
* **Delete Comments** – Remove inappropriate or unwanted discussions.

By enabling **Legacy Comments**, you foster engagement while maintaining **quality conversations** on your blog!

![](/images/builder/03906dc6e691681c.png)

### Member Comments 

Unlike **Legacy Comments**, **Member Comments** provide more control by allowing only **verified members** to comment on blog posts. This helps maintain a **secure and high-quality discussion space**.

#### **Key Features:**

* **Restricted Access** – Only verified members can post comments
* **Moderation Notifications** – Get alerts when new comments need review
* **Direct Replies** – Respond to comments within the moderation area

Enable **Member Comments** in your blog settings to foster **exclusive and meaningful interactions** within your community.

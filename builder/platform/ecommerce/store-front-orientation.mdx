#### **Streamlining Your Ecommerce Store Setup**

Your **main dashboard** provides quick access to essential features for efficient store design and inventory management.

* **Store Setup** – Configure foundational settings for seamless operation.
* **Creating Products** – Add and manage your product catalog effortlessly.
* **Adding Your Payment Processor** – Integrate secure payment gateways.
* **Store Details** – Adjust general information and settings.
* **Shipping Methods** – Define delivery options to suit your business needs.
* **Store Design** – Customize aesthetics for an engaging storefront.
* **Analytics** – Track performance metrics to refine your strategy.

A streamlined approach to **building and managing your online store effectively!**

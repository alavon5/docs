Easily **export your contacts** to an Excel spreadsheet for organization and analysis.

* **Go to the "More" Tab** – Access additional account settings.
* **Select "Export"** – Choose this option from the **drop-down menu** to initiate the process.
* **Download Your Contact List** – The data will populate your spreadsheet automatically.

A seamless way to manage and store your contact details efficiently!

![](/images/builder/24287cdf35a4d6e6.png)

Now a new window will open within the export section. To export your contacts - select export. Your contacts will now be added to your excel spreadsheet. Including all contact details from tags to membership data.

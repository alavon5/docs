To start receiving payments for your ecommerce products, follow these simple steps:

* **Select "Connect a Payment Processor"** – Navigate to the payment setup section.
* **Choose Your Preferred Processor** – Integrate options like Stripe, PayPal, or others.
* **Configure Payment Settings** – Set up currency preferences, transaction details, and security measures.

This ensures a **smooth checkout experience** for your customers while keeping payments secure!

![](/images/builder/8bbf9218c1e23668.png)

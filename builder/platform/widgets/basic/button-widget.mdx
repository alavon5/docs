Buttons are essential for guiding user interaction and creating seamless navigation.

* **Locate the Button Widget** – Open the **widget section** and find the **Button Widget** under **"Basic Widgets."**
* **Drag & Drop** – Place the button anywhere on your **page or container** for quick and easy integration.

Buttons can be used for **actions like navigation, submissions, and engagement**, ensuring a **smooth user experience**!

![](/images/builder/f9f9199401d75851.png)

When you have placed your button in the desired location, you're able to edit and modify the button settings.

![](/images/builder/65c9d49a2861aa58.png)

### **Editing**

Under the Edit tab, you can modify the button text, add a second line of text, add icons before or after the text, and attach a link to redirect the visitor after clicking the button.

![](/images/builder/91f9bc7510cdc82a.png)

### **Styling**

You can customize the button's visual display on your pages, including its color and animation.

![](/images/builder/ba187479786daa11.png)

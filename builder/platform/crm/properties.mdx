#### **Accessing Properties & Custom Properties**

Easily manage standard and custom properties for user profiles.

* **Navigate to the Settings Tab** – Open the settings panel in your dashboard.
* **Locate Properties & Custom Properties** – Adjust and configure key data points for personalization.

This ensures **streamlined profile customization** and **efficient data management**!

![](/images/builder/428bd7993b6aa953.png)

### Custom properties

Custom properties are really powerful and can aid your marketing efforts massively.

To create a custom property, simply select the field type from the list

**Field types:**

* Single line of text
* Email
* Number
* True/False
* Date
* List

![](/images/builder/b1660cf5f37b3fe1.png)

### **Available in forms**

If you require the field to be available in your forms, simply turn it on. Note: You can hide the fields too, under the hidden field type in your form settings.

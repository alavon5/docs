This widget displays a set of profile cards, photo, name, role, bio, and social links, in a variety of layouts. While designed for "Meet the Team" sections, it's flexible enough to use anywhere you need to showcase a group of people with consistent formatting.

![](/images/builder/f22c03f6ecf13f0f.png)

### **Why use it:**

* **Humanizes your brand**: putting real faces and names to a business builds trust and makes a company feel approachable rather than anonymous.
* **Versatile beyond team pages**: the same card format works well for speaker lineups, instructor/coach profiles, board members, contributor credits, ambassador/partner showcases, or "meet the experts" sections, anywhere you need a repeatable person + photo + title + bio format.
* **Connects visitors to real people**: social links let visitors follow up directly with the person featured, useful for sales teams, founders, or public-facing staff.
* **Multiple layouts for different contexts**: from a compact auto-rotating slider to a full team grid, so it fits both small "About us" blurbs and dedicated team pages.

### **Layout options:**

* **Slider**: rotates through profiles one at a time (or several per view). **Slider Type** options: Single slide, Multiple slides, or Auto rotate (no controls), plus a **Pause on hover** toggle.
* **List**: stacks profiles vertically with photo alongside name, role, bio, and socials, good for detailed team pages.
* **Grid**: displays profiles side by side in columns, with **Items per row** control, ideal for showing a full team at a glance.

### **How to add profiles:**

* Manage team members as a list, click **+ Add**, drag to reorder, click the gear to edit, or delete with the trash icon

![](/images/builder/e09982c1256b1f73.png)

### **Per-profile settings:**

* **Photo, Name, Role, Short Bio**
* **Social Links**: add multiple platforms (LinkedIn, X/Twitter, GitHub, Dribbble, Instagram, etc.) each with its own URL

![](/images/builder/a4c25f34fce1c27c.png)

### **Shared styling settings:**

* **Photo Shape**: Rounded or other shape options
* **Theme**: Light/Dark
* **Text Font Family:** typography control
* **Border Radius**: corner rounding on cards
* **Card background / Text Color / Accent Color**: full color customization

Easily integrate email services like **Mailchimp** and **ActiveCampaign** into your project for seamless communication.

**How to Add an Email Integration:**

1. **Go to Settings** – Click the **settings cog** in your dashboard.
2. **Select 'Applications'** – Navigate to the integrations menu.
3. **Choose Your Email Provider** – Pick the service you already use.

A **quick and hassle-free** way to streamline email automation!

![](/images/builder/47643e3ded4a6dbb.png)

Each provider will have it's own type of connection settings. Most of which are simply adding your API key provided by the provider it's self. Simply paste in the credentials and it will connect automatically for you.

### How to add people to your email lists 

Within the builder there are many options where you can add your visitors to you email marketing provider of choice. From users filling in forms to add them to your purchased campaigns via any checkout in your project.

Once your connection is successful you will see the additional option at the bottom of your[ forms](/builder/platform/widgets/lead-generation/forms-widget), checkout etc displaying the email provider you have connected. In this instance you can see that we have connected Mailerlite. Now whenever someone fill out that form they will be added to a selected Mailerlite email list.

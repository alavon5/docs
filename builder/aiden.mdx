AiDen is the AI helper built into Bosscart. It can answer questions in the context of the platform, guide you to the right area, help plan pages and funnels, troubleshoot from screenshots, and assist with selected business tasks.

You can type a request, use the microphone, or attach a screenshot when visual context would help.

![AiDen open in the Bosscart dashboard](/images/builder/aiden/aiden-help-panel.png)

<Note>
  AiDen is evolving. This guide describes its current capabilities; deeper business-aware assistance will be added over time.
</Note>

## How AiDen works

1. **Ask naturally.** Describe what you want to accomplish instead of searching for a specific command.
2. **Add useful context.** Mention the page, customer journey, product, or problem you are working on. Attach a screenshot when relevant.
3. **Review the response.** AiDen can guide, prepare, and assist, but you remain in control of changes to your site or business data.

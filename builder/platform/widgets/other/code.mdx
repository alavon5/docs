Adding custom code to your site is **quick and seamless**:

* **Go to Widgets** – Open the **Widgets panel** in your editor.
* **Navigate to "Other Widgets"** – Scroll to locate additional customization options.
* **Select the Code Widget** – Drag and drop it into your page or funnel.

![](/images/builder/d1dbe2ba1db1d331.png)

### **Edit Code**

Select your preferred code language format from the settings cog.

![](/images/builder/064d33e172e93f74.png)

**Edit Code**

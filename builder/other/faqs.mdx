**1. My Domain Is Connected, But It’s Not Working?**

🚀 **Solution:**

* Be patient—it can take **up to 24 hours** for domain propagation.
* If **more than 24 hours** have passed:
  * **Re-check your DNS settings** on your domain host.
  * **Remove & Re-add** the connection, as sometimes a fresh setup is needed.

**2. My Site Says It’s Not Secure?**

🔒 **Solution:**

* This is likely an **SSL certification issue**.
* Reach out with your **domain name**, and our team will **resolve it ASAP**!

**3. I’ve Reached My Subscriber Limit?**

📢 **Solution:**

* Congrats on **growing your subscriber list**!
* To remove the alert:
  * Head to your **billing area**.
  * Apply the **email plan** that suits the size of your list.

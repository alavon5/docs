Accessed via the settings cog on any page, this modal is where you control a page's core identity, access rules, and search engine presentation, split into two tabs: Page Info and SEO Info.

![](/images/builder/ee3e06deb3d2445d.png)

#### **Why use it:**

* **Controls who can see the page**, beyond just publishing publicly, you can restrict access for password-protected content or gated member-only areas, useful for premium content, client portals, or internal resources.
* **Better search visibility with less effort**, AI-assisted SEO means you don't need to be an SEO expert to write effective titles and descriptions; the AI can read your page content and generate them for you, or refine what you've already written.
* **Keeps site structure organized**, naming, grouping, and URL control help keep larger sites tidy as they grow.

![](/images/builder/7a699c0cb6a30cc6.png)

### **Page Info tab:**

* **Page Name**: internal/display name for the page
* **Page URL**: the page's slug (e.g., website.com/feature-demo-page)
* **Add to Group**: organize the page under a group/category for easier site navigation
* **Page Access**: control who can view the page:
  * **Public**: visible to anyone
  * **Password protected**: requires a password to view
  * **Members only**: restricted to members of a specific group
* **Use the alternative header for this page**: apply a different header than the site default
* **Set As Homepage**: make this the site's main landing page

![](/images/builder/92c1f224fb710db8.png)

### **SEO Info tab:**

* **Generate via AI**: automatically create a Page title and Meta Description by reading the page's content
* **Page title**: the title shown in search engine results and browser tabs (can also be AI-refined individually via the AI icon)
* **Meta Description**: the summary shown under your page title in search results (also AI-assistable)
* **Add to sitemap**: includes the page in your sitemap, improving search engine indexing
* **Share Image**: sets the image shown when this specific page is shared on social media (overrides the site-wide default)

Easily insert custom code into your website for **advanced functionality and integrations**.

* **Go to Widgets** – Open the **Widgets panel** in your editor.
* **Navigate to "Other Widgets"** – Locate additional customization options.
* **Select the Embed Widget** – Drag and drop it into your page for effortless code integration.

This enables full flexibility for embedding third-party tools, custom scripts, or HTML elements!

### **Adding your code**

Simply insert the embed code, click save and it will now be displayed in your builder.

To create a community, navigate to the top task bar and select the 'Community' tab. 

![](/images/builder/ef2cc7b3ee160b8e.png)

Once the community tab is selected, you will be able to create a **Circle**.

<Tip>
A Circle is a sub-community feature that allows you to create separate environments for your members. Depending on your plan, you can create a specified number of Circles, each allowing unlimited courses, pages, etc. For most users, one Circle will suffice, but you may opt for 2 or 3 Circles to accommodate different community types or offerings.
</Tip>

### Create a new Circle

To create a new or additional Circle, click the "+Add Circle" button. After selecting it, provide a name for the Circle and click "Save." Your new Circle is now ready

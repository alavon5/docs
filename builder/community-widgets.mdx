Community widgets function just like any other widget—you can **drag and drop** them into any **page, funnel, blog**, and yes, even within your **community Circles** for easy access and engagement.

They are conveniently located in the same **Widgets Area**, making it simple to find and integrate them into your layout without hassle.

This ensures a smooth and intuitive experience, allowing you to enhance your Circle with interactive features that align with your community’s needs.

![](/images/builder/6bab5202faaa73c2.png)<p>Community Widgets</p>

<Tip>
Remember: These community widgets can be used anywhere across your projects.
</Tip>

Customers have the **right to request or delete their personal data** stored and processed by you. To ensure compliance and transparency:

* **Provide a Request Form** – Create a dedicated form on your website where users can request their data.
* **Compile All Data** – Gather personal information stored across various platforms, including **CRMs, autoresponders, and our system**.

**Requesting Data from Our Servers**

* **Go to Website Settings** > **Cookies & Privacy** > **Request Customer Data** (bottom of the page).
* Within **72 hours**, our system will provide all available data for the requested customer (based on email).
* You can then **combine this with third-party data** for a complete response to the user.

This process ensures **privacy compliance** while giving users full control over their personal information.

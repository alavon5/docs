To enable email sending from your **custom domain**, you need to connect an **SMTP server** to your website.

**How to Set Up Your SMTP Server:**

1. **Go to Dashboard** – Access the main settings.
2. **Navigate to Applications** – Find the integration options.
3. **Select 'Email Server'** – Locate the SMTP configuration section.
4. **Click 'Connect'** – Initiate the setup process for sending emails from your domain.

A **straightforward way** to ensure professional and branded email communication!

![](/images/builder/34142434c0358346.png)

Then add the respective settings from your email hosting company in order to connect your mail to the website and allow it to send the emails.<br />

#### **Walls & Forums: Your New Community Hub**

This interactive space is where **members can connect, post, and engage freely.** **Walls & Forums** is the perfect **Facebook Group alternative** designed for **seamless discussions, dynamic interactions, and real-time engagement**.

**Key Features:**

**Make Posts** – Share updates, thoughts, or media-rich content.

**Comment & Reply** – Keep conversations flowing with structured discussions.

**Upvote & Downvote** – Members can express approval or provide constructive feedback.

**Like & Heart Reactions** – Engage with posts in a meaningful way.

**Poll Integration** – Create interactive polls directly within posts.

**Image Uploads** – Enhance posts with visual elements.

**Customization & Control** – Set permissions, adjust layouts, and fine-tune moderation options.

Whether you're building a **tight-knit community** or leading **open discussions**, **Walls & Forums** keeps engagement effortless while **offering full control** over content and interactions, without the constraints of traditional social platforms.

### **Main Settings & Display Options for Walls & Forums**

To ensure your **Walls & Forums** stay on brand, start by configuring the **main settings** and **display options** that best fit your community's structure.

**Wall Configuration Options:**

* **Single Wall** – A unified space where all posts, discussions, and interactions take place in **one central feed**.
* **Multiple Walls** – Organize content into **separate walls**, allowing different categories, topics, or member groups to have their own dedicated spaces.

This flexibility helps tailor the **engagement experience**, whether you prefer a **streamlined feed** or **structured discussions across multiple walls**.

![](/images/builder/37d57a9d7287e55e.png)

### **Naming Your Wall**

Before adding content, you’ll need to **name your wall** to ensure easy identification and consistency across your website projects.

**Options for Naming:**

* **Create a New Name** – Define a fresh title that fits the purpose of your wall.
* **Update an Existing Name** – Modify previously created wall names to align with updates.
* **Reuse a Name** – Select a name you’ve already used in other projects for branding consistency.

This step ensures seamless **management and organization**, allowing members to instantly recognize the focus of each wall.

### Post Layout

You can customize how **Wall Posts** appear by selecting one of these two display options:

* **Full Post** – Displays the entire post feed, including all text, images, and interactions **at a glance** for a more immersive experience.
* **Compact View** – Shows a **preview version** of the post, allowing users to quickly scan multiple posts without overwhelming the screen, and when they want to engage a neat pop out will be displayed for a more focused experience.

This flexibility ensures that your wall aligns with both **visual preferences** and **user engagement**.

If you prefer comments to appear **directly beneath the post**, you can adjust the display settings:

1. Navigate to the **Post Display Options** section.
2. Locate the setting **"Post will show in a popup on click."**
3. Switch the option to **"With Comments Below."**

This ensures that **post interactions remain visible**, keeping discussions fluid and accessible without requiring an additional click to view replies.

![](/images/builder/9eee8383121eb7b9.png)

If you prefer not to display comments when the **popup opens**, you can adjust the settings accordingly:

1. **Select the "Collapse Comments" option** in the post display settings.
2. When enabled, viewers will have the choice to **expand comments** either **in the sidebar** or **below the post**.

This setting keeps the post interface **clean and uncluttered**, while still allowing users to engage with discussions at their convenience.

### Post Approval

You have full control over post approvals, allowing you to **turn approval on or off** as needed.

* **When approval is required**, post will be held for review.
* You can **approve them in the moderation area**, ensuring only appropriate content is published.
* **Once approved, the comment will be published**, becoming visible to all users.

This feature helps maintain a well-moderated community while ensuring high-quality engagement.

[Community Moderation](/builder/community-lms/create-a-community/community-moderation)

### Post Comments

#### Initial Number of Comments to Display 

This straightforward setting allows you to **control how many comments** are visible by default before members need to load more.

* Choose to display **3, 5, 10, or even 20** comments initially.
* Ensures a **clean and organized** comment section layout.

This feature keeps engagement flexible while allowing for a streamlined experience.

![](/images/builder/05a196c1803fa33b.png)

### Approve Comments 

To review and approve comments before they’re published, follow these steps:

1. **Navigate to the Community tab** and select **Moderation**.
2. Within the moderation section, click **Awaiting Approval**.
3. Here, you will see all comments that are **awaiting approval or deletion**.
4. Approve the comment to publish it, or choose to delete it if necessary.

### Use Dark Theme 

Enabling the **dark theme** for post allows you to maintain a consistent brand aesthetic by switching from a **lighter theme** to a **darker** one.

* This option ensures **visual harmony** with your existing background color.
* Helps **enhance readability** while aligning with your design preferences.
* Allows for a **seamless user experience**, making posts and comments feel integrated with your overall layout.

Selecting the right theme depends on your **regular background color**, ensuring the post section complements your site’s design.

#### **Interactive & Engaging Chat Threads**

The **Discussions feature** is a powerful tool designed to enhance community interaction. It allows you to create **single or multiple chat threads**, enabling members to engage in dynamic conversations in real time.

**Key Features:**

* **Multi-threaded chats** – Organize conversations into distinct discussion topics.
* **Interactive polls** – Create polls that **trigger automations**, streamlining decision-making and engagement.
* **@ Mentions** – Tag members easily to **notify** them of important updates or direct replies.
* **Seamless navigation** – Quickly switch between **different chat threads** with a single click.

Think of **Discussions** as a more **interactive** and **structured** alternative to posts - similar to **Slack**, but more **on-brand** and optimized for **community engagement**.

<Tip>
Discussions are versatile and can be placed **anywhere** within your project, ensuring seamless integration across different content types. You can embed them into:

* **Pages** – Keep conversations relevant within structured content.
* **Blogs** – Encourage discussions around articles and shared insights.
* **Funnels** – Allow interaction within sales and conversion journeys.
* **Community Circles** – Foster engagement within dedicated member spaces.

This flexibility ensures members can participate **wherever it makes the most sense**, keeping discussions fluid and interactive across your platform.
</Tip>

### How to Create a Discussion

Firstly, navigate to the left-side taskbar and select the Discussion Widget. Then, simply drag and drop it to where you need it to display.

![](/images/builder/37ed5e2768e0e588.png)

Now that the **Discussion widget** has been added to the page, it’s time to explore customization options to ensure it fits seamlessly within your project.

**Key Areas to Edit:**

* **Thread Structure** – Adjust how discussions are organized and displayed.
* **Display Settings** – Customize visibility, themes, and layout preferences.
* **Interaction Controls** – Manage permissions for posts, replies, and mentions.
* **Automation & Polls** – Configure interactive elements to enhance engagement.

Let’s dive deeper into the **settings** and **display options** to refine the discussion experience.

### What Kind of Discussion Board do you want

When configuring your **Discussion Widget**, you can choose between displaying a **single channel** or **multiple discussion threads**.

**Starting with a Single Channel:**

* **Select a pre-designed thread** – Use an existing discussion layout that you’ve already created.
* **Update an existing thread** – Modify content, settings, or participant permissions.
* **Rename the channel** – Customize the name to align with the topic or purpose of the discussion.

### Discussion Board Name

You can add a title to your discussion board, which is a great option when using multiple threads, as it helps members easily engage with the right one.

<Tip>
Note: The Thread Title will only be displayed in specific display options, which we will cover below.
</Tip>

### Enter Channel Name

The **channel naming convention** ensures consistency across **all displays**, allowing seamless integration throughout your project.

* **Visible everywhere** – The assigned channel name appears across all sections where discussions are embedded.
* **Reusable threads** – You can use the same discussion thread in **pages, funnels, and circles** while keeping all attached chats intact.
* **Streamlined engagement** – Members can continue conversations across different areas without losing context.

This feature enhances organization and maintains a smooth discussion flow within your community setup.

### Creating Polls

#### **Interactive Polls: Gathering Insights & Feedback**

The **threads section** includes a powerful feature: **interactive polls**, allowing members to engage in discussions while providing valuable insights.

* **Gauge deeper opinions** – Gather feedback on specific topics to better understand community preferences.
* **Encourage participation** – Members can **vote and share their thoughts**, fostering engagement.
* **Automate actions** – Poll results can **trigger automations**, helping streamline decision-making.

This feature ensures discussions remain **dynamic and insightful**, making it easy to collect feedback in an interactive way.

![](/images/builder/aaa844beeeddd98e.png)<p>Poll Thread Comment Feature</p>

### Poll Settings

When creating polls, you have **complete control** over how members interact, ensuring polls align with your specific needs.

* **Set commenting permissions** – Decide who can comment on poll discussions.
* **Allow option additions** – Enable members to contribute additional poll choices, letting them add their input.
* **Encourage engagement** – Empower your community to shape discussions with personalized responses.

This flexibility allows for a **dynamic and interactive polling experience**, fostering deeper insights and collaboration.

![](/images/builder/2d4f442a9584d0a6.png)

#### **How many answers to the poll**

This option allows you to enable members to either select a single answer or multiple answers in your poll.

#### Who can add more poll options

When setting up your poll, you have **three options** for allowing additional answers:

* **Only You** – Only the poll creator can add new options.
* **Moderators Only** – Moderators have the ability to contribute additional choices.
* **Everyone** – All members can freely add their own poll options.

<Tip>
Note: You can go back in to any of your threads at anytime and modify any settings.
</Tip>

## Multiple Channel Threads

With **multiple channel discussion threads**, you can host **more than one thread** at a time, allowing diverse topics to run simultaneously while maintaining a clean and customizable layout.

**Getting Started:**

1. **Navigate to the 'What kind of discussion board do you want' tab.**
2. **Select the 'Multiple Channels' option.**
3. This enables **various threads** to be displayed at once, each with its own unique topic and structure.
4. **Customize the appearance** to ensure it fits seamlessly within your project’s design.

This setup is perfect for managing **distinct discussions**, encouraging engagement across different areas while keeping conversations well-organized.

![](/images/builder/26e26d8f5be598dd.png)<p>Multiple Channel Threads</p>

### Styling Multiple Channel Threads

* **Show Group Background Color** – Customize the **background color** to maintain branding consistency.

These features help create a **cohesive and engaging** discussion space that fits seamlessly into your platform's design.

### Add an Additional Thread

To create an additional thread within your **Discussion Board**, follow these steps:

1. **Navigate to the Thread Box** labeled **"Define the channels of your discussion board."**
2. **Select "Add Channel"** to open a new thread option.
3. **Enter Channel Name** - Select an existing thread or create new
4. **Edit your styling** - As mentioned and shown above.

### View Style

Now that you've created **two threads**, you can choose a **display type** that best fits your layout and user experience.

**View Style Options:**

1. **Row View** – Displays threads in a **stacked layout**, offering a simple and structured flow.
2. **Column View** – Organizes threads into **contained boxes**, visually separating topics for easy navigation.
3. **Sidebar View** – Presents threads in a **chat-like dashboard**, mimicking a real-time messaging experience.

Each style serves a unique purpose, ensuring flexibility in how members engage with discussions.

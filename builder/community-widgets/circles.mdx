<Tip>
Just so you're not confused, Circles are a collection of an individual Circle.
</Tip>

Circles act as **groups of individual Circles**, allowing communities to be structured with multiple interconnected spaces. Each Circle retains its own content, engagement features, and customization, while Circles as a whole provide a larger framework for organization.

This setup makes it easy to **manage multiple communities**, ensuring streamlined navigation and a cohesive experience for members.

![](/images/builder/b139a556afc96029.png)<p>1 Circle added to a standard page</p>

#### **Organizing Multiple Circles with Categories**

If you have multiple Circles, you can **organize them into categories**, making it easier for members to navigate and find relevant content within your community.

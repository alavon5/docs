### How to Set a Publishing Date for Your Post/Article 

Go to **'Post Date'** in the [article settings menu](/builder/blog/article-settings) and select the date and time for the article.

![](/images/builder/db709b90ead0a9bd.png)

### How to Schedule a Post/Article to Publish at a Future Date 

Easily schedule blog posts by setting a **future date and time** in the ‘Post Date’ field.

**How to Schedule a Post:**

1. **Navigate to the Article Menu** – Open the post settings.
2. **Locate ‘Post Date’** – Enter the desired publication date and time.
3. **Save Your Changes** – The system will **automatically publish** the post at the scheduled time.

A **hassle-free way** to plan content ahead and maintain a consistent posting schedule!

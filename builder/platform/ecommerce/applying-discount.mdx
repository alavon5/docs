Boost sales and incentivize purchases with **three types of discounts** you can configure:

* **Flat Discount** – Deduct a fixed amount from the total price.
* **Percentage Discount** – Apply a percentage-based reduction on the purchase.
* **Free Shipping** – Remove shipping costs to encourage more conversions.

**Getting Started**

* **Navigate to the Discount Tab** – Located in **Store Settings**.
* **Enter Your Required Discount Information** – Set up rules tailored to your business needs.

A great way to **increase customer engagement and drive conversions effortlessly!**

![](/images/builder/0ea89b05af75032c.png)

### **Adding discount to specific variations.** 

At the bottom of the pop-out window, you can select where you wish to apply the discount. Simply select your product, variation, or even allocated membership groups.

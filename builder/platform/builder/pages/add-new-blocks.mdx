### Add new blocks

Blocks make it easy to **assemble visually stunning pages** without needing advanced design skills.

* **Hundreds of Pre-Designed Options** – Choose from a wide variety of blocks tailored for blogs, portfolios, business sites, and more.
* **Seamless Integration** – Each block is designed for **effortless customization** and smooth alignment within your project.
* **Balanced Functionality & Aesthetics** – Prioritize content while maintaining a sleek, polished layout.

**Adding a New Block**

* Navigate to the **top or bottom** of any container.
* Select the **block you want to insert** for quick and intuitive page-building.

This approach allows for **easy creativity**, ensuring your site looks professional with minimal effort!

![](/images/builder/77f02d4da48373ed.png)

Once you select the block tab, you'll see a gallery of pre-designed blocks. Choose the one you like, and it will be added to your page for editing.

![](/images/builder/f2130048e6ee554e.png)

Your block has been added to the page and is now ready for editing.

![](/images/builder/fec74afe7017a546.png)

You can also save your block design for future use or for another project.

Go to the top of the block and select "Copy Widget":

![](/images/builder/3e63cbfa2cbba46a.png)

After selecting the copy widget, your copied block will appear under the saved blocks icon.

![](/images/builder/0bc4e92b256c9f41.png)

To save the container for future use, click the cog icon next to it, name it, and save. You can find it later under the saved tab.

![](/images/builder/19d861e15318a848.png)
